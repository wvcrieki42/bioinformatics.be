Key takeaways

  • Simple statistics like GC content already carry biological signal.
  • CpG dinucleotides underpin epigenetic regulation; k-mers generalise the idea.
  • Chaos Game Representation turns sequence composition into recognisable visual patterns.
  • AI foundation models are an emerging, powerful way to learn the genome's language.
