05 Chapter Five

Database Searching

You have a sequence; the database has millions. BLAST, FASTA and BWA find the needles fast — by seeding on short words and extending the promising hits, with statistics to tell signal from noise.

⚡

Seed & extend

BLAST finds short exact "word" matches, then extends them into high-scoring pairs.

📐

The E-value

How many hits this good you'd expect by chance — the lower, the more significant.

🧬

BWA & the BWT

The Burrows-Wheeler transform indexes a genome so short reads map in a flash.

How BLAST works: seed, then extend

A query is broken into words; exact word hits in the database seed local alignments that extend until the score drops.

Significance: where does your hit fall?

Scores from random hits follow an extreme-value distribution; a real hit sits far in the tail.

The Burrows-Wheeler transform

Reversible reordering that clusters characters — the basis of fast read mapping in BWA.

Key takeaways

  • BLAST trades guaranteed optimality for speed using a seed-and-extend heuristic.
  • The E-value reports how many equally good hits you'd expect by chance — treat a search like an experiment.
  • FASTA and BLAST differ in seeding strategy; both report local alignments with statistics.
  • The Burrows-Wheeler transform powers aligners like BWA for mapping millions of short reads.
